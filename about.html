WHO WE ARE

A School Built Around Every Child

Quthing Inclusive International Primary School is an inclusive school that also accommodates children with special needs and disabilities. We believe that every child deserves an opportunity to learn, grow and participate in a safe, caring and supportive school environment.

We believe that every learner is unique and deserves opportunities to discover their strengths and develop their abilities.
