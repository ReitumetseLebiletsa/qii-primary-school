About Our School

Discover who we are, what we believe and what makes QII Primary School a special learning community.

WHO WE ARE

A School Built Around Every Child

Quthing Inclusive International Primary School is committed to providing quality education in a safe, welcoming and supportive environment.

We believe that every learner is unique. Every child deserves the opportunity to learn, discover their strengths and develop their abilities.

Our school seeks to create an environment where learners can grow academically, socially, emotionally and creatively.

We believe that education is not only about what happens inside the classroom. It is also about building character, confidence, responsibility and respect.

OUR DIRECTION

Our Mission & Vision

Our Mission

To provide quality and inclusive education that helps every learner develop knowledge, confidence, character and the skills needed to grow and succeed.

Our Vision

To nurture confident, responsible and capable young people who are prepared to make a positive contribution to their families, communities and the wider world.

Inclusion

We value every learner and believe that children should have opportunities to participate, learn and develop in a supportive school environment.

Growth

We encourage learners to keep learning, discovering their abilities and developing the confidence to face new challenges.

OUR VALUES

What We Believe In

These values guide the way we teach, learn and work together as a school community.

Excellence

We encourage learners to always give their best and take pride in their work.

Respect

We treat every member of our school community with dignity, kindness and understanding.

Teamwork

We believe children thrive when teachers, parents and learners work together.

Creativity

We encourage curiosity, imagination, problem-solving and new ideas.

Responsibility

We help learners understand the importance of responsibility and positive choices.

Community

We value strong relationships between the school, families and the wider community.
