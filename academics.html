OUR APPROACH

Learning That Builds Strong Foundations

At Quthing Inclusive International Primary School, we believe that quality education should help children develop both knowledge and confidence.

Our learning environment encourages learners to ask questions, solve problems, communicate their ideas and discover the world around them.

We also believe that academic development works best when learners, teachers and parents work together.

Science

Encouraging curiosity, observation and understanding of the natural world.

Creative Arts
